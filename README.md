# Flipped (2010)

## About the Movie
*Flipped* is a coming-of-age romantic drama film directed by **Rob Reiner** and based on the novel by **Wendelin Van Draanen**. The story follows Juli Baker and Bryce Loski as they grow up and learn valuable lessons about friendship, family, and seeing people beyond first impressions.

## Main Characters
- Juli Baker
- Bryce Loski
- Richard Baker
- Steven Loski

## Why It's My Favorite Movie
1. It has a heartwarming story.
2. The characters show personal growth.
3. It teaches important life lessons.
4. The movie presents both characters' perspectives.

## Favorite Quote
> "The whole is greater than sum of its parts." Richard (Juli's father) explains Juli to look at a painting, not by just the subject, but by extension, the world, and the people.  

## Genre
- Romance
- Drama
- Comedy

## Rating
**10/10**

### Personal Reflection
I enjoy *Flipped* because it teaches viewers to look beyond appearances and appreciate people for who they truly are. The movie shows the importance of kindness, understanding, and staying true to oneself.
